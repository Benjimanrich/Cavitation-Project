First the screenshot was calibrated using 4 points on the axes, the first 2 for the origin and max ranges of the x-axis and the next 2 for origin and max range of the y-axis

then the remaining points were marked using the multipoint tool while maintaining the previous 4 selections.

Then the result was viewed and the corresponding macro applied(top left or top right plot).The macro was generated by an LLM

The selections and calibration were done differently for each plot(hence the two separate macros)

the final results from each plot were exported as csv files, these were manually cleaned up to get the final data set.
